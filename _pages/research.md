My research identifies the causal effects of existing and proposed policies on diet, food security, obesity, and cardiometabolic health, with an emphasis on reducing health disparities. I draw on quasi-experimental and experimental designs (difference-in-differences, interrupted time series, randomized trials), microsimulation-based cost-effectiveness analysis, and machine learning, and I work closely with government and community partners so that findings inform real decisions.

### Nutrition assistance programs

How SNAP, WIC, and school meals support children and families, and how program design (benefit levels, remote services, administrative burden) shapes participation, food security, and diet.

- Remote WIC services and benefit issuance increased participation and retention — [_JAMA Netw Open_ 2026](/publications/#kenney2026remote), [_Health Affairs_ 2026](/publications/#dore2026wic)
- Larger WIC fruit and vegetable benefits were redeemed at high rates — [_Pediatrics Open Science_ 2026](/publications/#kenney2026increases)
- Statewide universal school meals and food insecurity — [_Pediatrics_ 2026](/publications/#kinderknecht2026statewide)
- The 2021 SNAP benefit increase, food security, and diet quality — [_Nutrients_ 2026](/publications/#poole2026changes)
- Simplified SNAP recertification and continuous benefit access — [_JAMA Netw Open_ 2022](/publications/#kenney2022simplification)
- Caregivers' perspectives on WIC and SNAP together — [_AJPH_ 2025](/publications/#gago2025struggle)

### Cash transfers and anti-poverty policy

Whether income support, paid leave, and tax credits improve health, and how to help eligible families take up the benefits they are owed.

- A randomized guaranteed income program improved diet quantity and quality in Chelsea, MA — [_Nature Food_ 2026](/publications/#lee2026randomized)
- State paid family leave and perinatal health nationwide — [_Pediatrics_ 2026](/publications/#lee2026impacts)
- Comparing economic assistance models for food security and diet quality — [_Health Affairs_ 2025](/publications/#poole2025understanding)
- Behavioral strategies to increase Earned Income Tax Credit take-up among California WIC participants — [under review](/publications/#lee2026behavioral)

### Sugar-sweetened beverage taxes and food marketing

Evaluations of local beverage taxes in Berkeley, Oakland, San Francisco, and Philadelphia, and how marketing and retail environments respond.

- Consumption three years after the Berkeley tax — [_AJPH_ 2019](/publications/#lee2019sugar)
- Retail price pass-through in Oakland and San Francisco — [_AJPH_ 2020](/publications/#falbe2020higher)
- Advertising and stocking at small retailers after the Philadelphia tax — [_AJPM_ 2024](/publications/#lee2024advertising)
- Beverage ad spending after the Philadelphia tax — [_AJPM_ 2024](/publications/#hua2024philadelphia)
- Socioeconomic disparities in outdoor beverage advertising — [_Prev Med Rep_ 2022](/publications/#zahid2022socioeconomic)

### Cost-effectiveness and microsimulation

Projecting the long-run health, equity, and cost impacts of policy using the Childhood Obesity Interventions Cost-Effectiveness Study (CHOICES) microsimulation model.

- A statewide sugar-sweetened beverage excise tax in California — [_AJPM_ 2024](/publications/#lee2024sugar)
- An improved WIC food package for preventing childhood obesity — [_Pediatrics_ 2024](/publications/#kenney2024cost)

### Diet quality across the life course

Trends and determinants of diet quality from toddlerhood through adolescence, and tools for measuring it.

- Changes in Healthy Eating Index-2020 scores and BMI among adolescents — [_J Nutr_ 2026](/publications/#lee2026longitudinal)
- Trends in toddler diet quality, 1999–2018 — [_Pediatrics_ 2024](/publications/#zimmer2024trends)
- `dietaryindex`, an R package for standardized dietary pattern analysis — [_AJCN_ 2024](/publications/#zhan2024dietaryindex)

### Policy evaluation in government

As a fellow with the Harvard Kennedy School Government Performance Lab, I evaluated local government programs for implementation, burden, scalability, and resident wellbeing — including the behavioral health effects of alternative emergency response in Los Angeles (using BERT and other language models on unstructured call text) and the causes of homelessness in Detroit, to improve housing crisis call scripts and referrals for residents facing eviction.
