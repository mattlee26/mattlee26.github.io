research

Research areas and selected papers.

My research looks at how food and social policies affect diet, food security, obesity, and cardiometabolic health, and whether they reduce health disparities. I rely on quasi-experimental designs such as difference-in-differences (DiD) methods and interrupted time series models. I also leverage data from randomized experiments and build microsimulation models for cost-effectiveness analysis. My work is informed by the decisions made by government agencies and policymakers.

Nutrition assistance programs

I study SNAP, WIC, and school meals, including how benefit amounts, remote services, and paperwork requirements affect participation, food security, and diet.

  • Remote WIC services and remote benefit issuance increased participation and retention (JAMA Netw Open 2026; Health Affairs 2026)
  • Families redeemed most of the increased WIC fruit and vegetable benefit (Pediatrics Open Science 2026)
  • Statewide universal school meals and food insecurity (Pediatrics 2026)
  • The 2021 SNAP benefit increase, food security, and diet quality in Massachusetts (Nutrients 2026)
  • Simpler SNAP recertification and uninterrupted access to benefits (JAMA Netw Open 2022)
  • Caregivers’ views on using WIC and SNAP together (AJPH 2025)

Cash transfers and anti-poverty policy

I study whether income support and paid leave improve health, and how to help eligible families claim benefits like the Earned Income Tax Credit.

  • Unconditional cash transfers improved diet quantity and quality in a randomized study in Chelsea, MA (Nature Food 2026)
  • State paid family leave and perinatal health (Pediatrics 2026)
  • A rapid review of economic assistance models for food security and diet quality (Health Affairs 2025)
  • A randomized pilot of behavioral strategies to increase EITC take-up among California WIC participants (under review)

Sugar-sweetened beverage taxes and food marketing

I have evaluated local sweetened beverage taxes in Berkeley, Oakland, San Francisco, and Philadelphia, looking at consumption, prices, store stocking, and advertising.

  • Consumption three years after the Berkeley tax (AJPH 2019)
  • Retail prices after the Oakland and San Francisco taxes (AJPH 2020)
  • Advertising and stocking at small retailers after the Philadelphia tax (AJPM 2024)
  • Beverage ad spending after the Philadelphia tax (AJPM 2024)
  • Socioeconomic disparities in outdoor beverage advertising (Prev Med Rep 2022)

Cost-effectiveness and microsimulation

I have used the Childhood Obesity Interventions Cost-Effectiveness Study (CHOICES) microsimulation model to estimate the long-term health effects and costs of proposed policies, and I developed the R code that processes the model’s output.

  • A statewide sugar-sweetened beverage excise tax in California (AJPM 2024)
  • An improved WIC food package to prevent childhood obesity (Pediatrics 2024)

Diet quality in children and adolescents

  • Changes in Healthy Eating Index-2020 scores and BMI among adolescents (J Nutr 2026)
  • Trends in toddler diet quality, 1999 to 2018 (Pediatrics 2024)
  • dietaryindex, an R package for calculating dietary pattern scores (AJCN 2024)
